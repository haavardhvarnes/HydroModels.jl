Reading
-------

The headline change is that **17 phantom virtual reservoir-to-reservoir
tunnels were suppressed**. These tunnels were emitted by the previous
parser for non-dominant intakes of multi-source plants — e.g. Logga →
Eikrebekken (Hemsil2's intake set) — to fake the mass balance when the
LP could only debit one source reservoir. With B1.6's per-(generator,
source) flow-split variable `Qg_from[u, s, j]` the LP now debits each
intake reservoir directly, so the phantom routes are unnecessary and
removing them eliminates an LP-degeneracy.

Multi-source plants in this case (NO5 PreSpot) according to the audit:

  Plant              Source reservoirs (sorted by capacity desc)
  -----              --------------------------------------------
  Aurland2HøyeFall   StoreVargevatn, Svartavatn, Langavatn, Katlavatn,
                     Berdalsvatn                            (5 sources)
  Hemsil1            Flævatn, Dyrja, Fagerdøla              (3 sources)
  Hemsil2            Eikrebekken, Logga, Ruståni            (3 sources)
  Hol1Urunda         Sisseldøla + Greinefoss + Strandevatn
                     (via nested junctions)                 (3 sources)
  Aurland2LaveFall   Rausmesdalen, Vetlebotn                (2 sources)
  Usta               Ustevatn, RødungenSør                  (2 sources)

Reservoirs feeding any of the above now correctly deplete in proportion
to the LP-chosen per-source flow allocation, bounded by their respective
tunnel-chain capacities.

The **objective drops by 0.02 %** (612.96 vs 613.08 MEUR). This is the
combined effect of:

- Energy revenue going **up by 1 %** (+0.054 MEUR) — the LP now turbines
  ~1350 MWh more by using each upstream's water value cuts properly
  rather than artificially routing water through the dominant source.
- End-of-horizon water values dropping by ~0.17 MEUR — multiple intake
  reservoirs depleting a little each is valued less than one reservoir
  staying full while the rest stay full too (water-value cuts are
  per-reservoir scalar slopes; aggregate sensitivity changes when the
  attribution is correct).

The **total spill is unchanged** (3903 m³/s·tsteps) — the cascade's
terminal mass-balance dictates the spill total, and the routing fix
doesn't change which reservoirs are terminal. What does change:
**Eikrebekken's share drops** (850 → 752) because phantom inflow from
Logga / Ruståni is no longer being routed to it. **Ruståni gains a
small explicit spill** (63.8) — water that previously "vanished" into
Eikrebekken via the phantom tunnel now correctly stays at Ruståni
when the per-source tunnel cap is full and the natural inflow exceeds
it.

The per-reservoir attribution story is the real win. Storage trajectories
for the 13 distinct intake reservoirs of multi-source plants now reflect
their actual water-value bindings instead of being lumped into the
dominant intake. That makes the LP's per-reservoir water-value cuts
(extracted from SDDP) actionable — operators can read each reservoir's
trajectory and see the LP's decision per reservoir, not just the
aggregate-via-dominant.
