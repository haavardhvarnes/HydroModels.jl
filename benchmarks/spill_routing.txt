Reading
-------

The objective and total spill are bit-identical because the routing only
shifts where the spilled water lands — it doesn't open up a new
generation path for the LP. In this case, Flatsjø + Ruståni + Logga's
spill (~691 m³/s·tsteps combined) now flows into Eikrebekken's mass
balance (parser inferred Eikrebekken as their tunnel-downstream).
Eikrebekken has no outgoing tunnel or generator that the LP can use,
so it spills the routed water on. Net result: the same total mass is
"lost" at Eikrebekken (now: 159 + 691 ≈ 850 m³/s·tsteps) instead of
disappearing piecemeal at Flatsjø / Ruståni / Logga. Limarka was and
still is a true terminal node — no outgoing tunnel or generator in
the parsed model.

The B1.5 fix is a **correctness improvement, not a performance one**
for this specific case. The spillover bookkeeping is now physically
honest: water entering the cascade either exits through a turbine
(captured in `Qg`), through a generator's pump (`Qpump`), through an
explicit river tunnel (`Qtun`), or it overflows downstream until it
reaches a terminal node and spills out there. Before B1.5, water that
overtopped any upstream dam was silently deleted from the system.

The user-facing implication: the "Top spillers" diagnostic from
`scripts/run_pipeline.jl` is now interpretable as
**"where is water actually leaving the system?"** rather than
**"where in the cascade is local capacity binding?"**. The post-B1.5
list (Limarka + Eikrebekken) identifies the two genuine terminal
nodes; the operational fix on the SHOP side would be to add a
downstream tunnel from one of those two if more water can be captured
in practice.
